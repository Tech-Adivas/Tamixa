# Tamixa Casual Naming Strategy - Easy & Friendly

## Philosophy
Use **everyday words** that everyone uses in daily conversation - from 6-year-olds to 60-year-olds. No fancy Sanskrit, no formal terms. Just simple, friendly names.

## Story Categories (Simple & Clear)

### 1. Digital Safety / Learning → **Innovate & Learn** / **Do Something New**

**Philosophy**: Action-oriented, empowering language that encourages kids to think creatively about learning and safety. Each language uses everyday conversational words that inspire innovation.

| Language | Translation | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|----------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Tamil | புதுமை செய் | Pudhumai Sei | Innovate / Do something new |
| Kannada | ಹೊಸತು ಮಾಡು | Hosathu Maadu | Do something new |
| Malayalam | പുതുമ കണ്ടെത്തൂ | Puthuma Kandethu | Find/Create novelty |
| Telugu | కొత్తగా చేయి | Kotthaga Cheyi | Do it in a new way |
| Hindi | कुछ नया करो | Kuch naya karo | Do something new |
| English | Be Smart | Be Smart | Be Smart |

**Why these translations work:**
- **Action verbs**: All use imperative forms (செய், ಮಾಡು, കണ്ടെത്തൂ, చేయి, करो) - telling kids to DO something
- **Casual & conversational**: Words parents and kids use daily
- **Empowering**: Focus on creation and innovation, not just safety
- **Age-appropriate**: 6-year-olds to 60-year-olds understand these words

## Key Principles

### ✅ Do's
- Use words kids and adults both use daily
- Keep it conversational ("Ippo Hit" not "Trending")
- Mix English words that everyone knows (Business, Leader, Net)
- Make it sound like a friend talking
- Use questions for ethics stories ("Right or Wrong?")

### ❌ Don'ts
- No formal Sanskrit words (Payanam → Home)
- No literary language (Katha Manjari → Stories)
- No academic terms (Suraksha Chakra → Stay Safe Online)
- No words that need explanation
- No words that sound preachy

---

## Why This Works Better

### **For Kids (6-12)**
- "Stories" is clearer than "Katha Kosh"
- "Home" is simpler than "Payanam"
- "Hot Now" is cooler than "Lokpriya"

### **For Teens (13-17)**
- "Money Smart" sounds aspirational
- "Be a Leader" is empowering
- "Right or Wrong?" engages them

### **For Parents**
- "Stay Safe Online" is clear and urgent
- "Know Your Rights" is practical
- "Health Tips" is straightforward

### **For Grandparents**
- Simple words they use daily
- No confusion about meaning
- Easy to remember and say

**Use this casual naming strategy** because:
1. ✅ Everyone understands immediately

4. ✅ Sounds friendly, not corporate

6. ✅ Memorable and shareable
