# Tamixa Multilingual Naming Strategy

## Supported Languages
- **Tamil** (ta) - தமிழ்
- **Kannada** (ka) - ಕನ್ನಡ
- **Malayalam** (ml) - മലയാളം
- **Telugu** (te) - తెలుగు
- **Hindi** (hi) - हिन्दी
- **English** (en)

## Naming Philosophy
Use **pan-Indian Sanskrit/common words** that are understood across all South Indian languages and Hindi, avoiding language-specific terms that might alienate users.

## Implementation Notes

### Key Principles
1. **Use Sanskrit-origin words** when possible (understood across all languages)
2. **Keep it simple** - 1-2 words maximum
3. **Test pronunciation** - ensure it's easy to say in all languages
4. **Cultural sensitivity** - avoid words with negative connotations in any language
5. **Consistency** - use the same root word across languages where possible

### Translation Priority
1. **High Priority** (Week 1): Main navigation, story categories
2. **Medium Priority** (Week 2): Dashboard sections, profile names
3. **Low Priority** (Week 3): Settings, tooltips, error messages

### Quality Assurance
- Native speaker review for each language
- Pronunciation testing with users
- Cultural appropriateness check
- A/B testing with sample users in each language

## Cultural Notes

### Tamil (ta)
- Prefer pure Tamil words over Sanskrit when possible
- "Kathai" (கதை) is universally understood for "story"
- Avoid overly formal/literary Tamil

### Kannada (ka)
- Sanskrit-origin words are well-accepted
- "Kathe" (ಕಥೆ) for story is common
- Mix of Sanskrit and native Kannada works well

### Malayalam (ml)
- Heavy Sanskrit influence is natural
- "Katha" (കഥ) is standard for story
- Formal Malayalam is acceptable for education

### Telugu (te)
- Sanskrit words are prestigious
- "Katha" (కథ) is universal for story
- Formal Telugu is preferred for education

### Hindi (hi)
- Sanskrit-origin words are standard
- "Kahani" (कहानी) is more colloquial than "Katha"
- Mix of Urdu and Sanskrit is natural

### English (en)
- Keep it simple and modern
- Avoid overly academic terms
- Make it memorable and unique
