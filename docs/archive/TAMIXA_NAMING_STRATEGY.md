# Tamixa Naming Strategy - Unique & Professional

## Brand Philosophy
Tamixa is not just another edutainment app. We're building **India's first AI-powered life skills companion** that prepares children and families for real-world challenges through immersive storytelling.

## Core Naming Principles

### 1. **Avoid Generic Terms**
❌ Library, Simulator, Dashboard, Profile  
✅ Unique, culturally resonant, memorable names

### 2. **Indian Cultural Resonance**
Use Sanskrit/Hindi words that convey wisdom, growth, and journey without being preachy

### 3. **Age-Appropriate Tone**
- Kids (6-12): Playful, adventurous
- Teens (13-17): Aspirational, empowering
- Adults: Sophisticated, practical

### 4. **Memorable & Pronounceable**
Easy to say, spell, and remember across languages

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Do's
✅ Use culturally resonant Hindi/Sanskrit terms  
✅ Keep names short (2-3 words max)  
✅ Make it pronounceable in English and Hindi  
✅ Test with parents and kids before finalizing  
✅ Ensure names work across age groups  

### Don'ts
❌ Use overly academic or preachy terms  
❌ Copy competitor naming (Spotify, Netflix patterns)  
❌ Use English-only generic terms  
❌ Make it too complex or hard to remember  
❌ Ignore regional language sensitivities

## Cultural Notes

- **Safar** (सफ़र): Urdu/Hindi word for journey, widely understood across India
- **Katha** (कथा): Sanskrit for story, used in religious and folk traditions
- **Suraksha** (सुरक्षा): Hindi for protection/safety, universally recognized
- **Dhan** (धन): Sanskrit for wealth, positive connotation
- **Dharma** (धर्म): Sanskrit for righteousness/duty, deeply cultural
- **Nyaya** (न्याय): Sanskrit for justice, formal but accessible

All names have been chosen to be:
- Pronounceable in English
- Meaningful in Hindi/Sanskrit
- Culturally appropriate
- Age-neutral
- Memorable and unique
