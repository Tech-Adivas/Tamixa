# Tamixa Mobile App User Guide

## Welcome to Tamixa Premium

Tamixa is your child's personalized storytelling companion, featuring immersive stories, interactive adventures, and educational content designed to inspire learning and imagination.

## Getting Started

### First Launch: Onboarding

When you first open Tamixa, you'll experience a guided tour:

1. **Welcome Screen**: Learn about Tamixa's storytelling magic
2. **Story Preview**: See sample stories and how they work
3. **Interactive Stories**: Discover branching adventures where choices matter
4. **Voice Setup** (Optional): Record your voice for personalized narration
5. **Avatar Setup** (Optional): Upload a photo for custom avatars

**Tip**: You can skip any step and set it up later in Settings.

### Navigation

Tamixa uses a bottom navigation bar with four main sections:

- **Dashboard** (Home icon): Your personalized story feed
- **Library** (Book icon): Browse all available stories
- **Generate** (Plus icon): Create custom stories
- **Profile** (Person icon): View progress and settings

## Dashboard

Your Dashboard is your storytelling home base.

### Greeting Card

At the top, you'll see:
- Personalized greeting based on time of day
- Current listening streak (🔥 icon)
- Monthly story usage

### Spotlight Stories

Featured stories handpicked by our team, updated weekly.

**How to use**:
- Swipe left/right to browse
- Tap any story to start listening

### Story Categories

Stories organized by theme:
- **Adventure**: Exciting journeys and quests
- **Learning**: Educational stories teaching life skills
- **Fun**: Entertaining tales for pure enjoyment
- **Safety**: Stories about staying safe and making good choices

**How to use**:
- Scroll down to see all categories
- Swipe within each row to see more stories
- Tap any story card to open the player

### Pull to Refresh

Swipe down from the top to refresh your dashboard with the latest content.

## Library

Browse Tamixa's complete story collection.

### Hub Tabs

Stories are organized into four hubs:

1. **Browse**: All library stories
2. **Fun Corner**: Entertainment and adventure stories
3. **Learn & Safety**: Educational content and life skills
4. **Simulator**: Interactive decision-making stories

**How to use**:
- Tap any hub tab to filter stories
- Scroll to browse available stories
- Use the search icon (🔍) to find specific stories

### Filters

Refine your search:
- **Language**: Tamil, English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam
- **Reading Level**: Age-appropriate content
- **Duration**: Short (5-10 min), Medium (10-20 min), Long (20+ min)

### Story Cards

Each story card shows:
- Cover illustration
- Story title
- Category badge
- Duration
- Interactive badge (for branching stories)
- Progress indicator (if partially listened)

## Story Player

The immersive listening experience.

### Player Controls

- **Play/Pause**: Start or pause playback
- **Skip Back** (⏪): Jump back 15 seconds
- **Skip Forward** (⏩): Jump ahead 15 seconds
- **Previous/Next**: Navigate between story segments

### Progress Bar

- Shows current position and total duration
- Drag to seek to any position
- Automatically saves your progress

### Speed Control

Adjust playback speed:
- **0.75x**: Slower for younger listeners
- **1.0x**: Normal speed (default)
- **1.25x**: Slightly faster
- **1.5x**: Faster for experienced listeners

**How to change**: Tap the speed indicator (e.g., "1.0x") to cycle through options.

### Background Playback

Stories continue playing when you:
- Switch to another app
- Lock your device
- Receive a phone call (pauses automatically)

**Lock Screen Controls**:
- Play/pause
- Skip forward/back
- Story artwork and title

### Related Stories

At the bottom of the player, discover similar stories based on:
- Same category
- Similar themes
- Same narrator

## Story Generation

Create personalized stories for your child.

### Step 1: Select Child

Choose which child the story is for (affects personalization and reading level).

### Step 2: Choose Theme

Select from popular themes:
- Adventure & Exploration
- Learning & Education
- Friendship & Social Skills
- Safety & Life Skills
- Fantasy & Imagination

### Step 3: Customize

Add details:
- Story length (short, medium, long)
- Specific topics or lessons
- Character preferences

### Step 4: Generate

Watch as AI creates your story:
- Real-time progress updates
- Estimated time remaining
- Cancel option if needed

**Generation Time**: Typically 30-60 seconds

### After Generation

Your story is automatically:
- Saved to your library
- Ready to play immediately
- Available in your selected language

**Subscription Limits**: Free users get 3 stories/month, Premium users get unlimited.

## Profile

Manage your account and track progress.

### User Information

- Name and nickname
- Avatar image
- Email address
- Edit profile button

### Learning Progress

Track your child's development:

**Reading Level**: Current level (1-10)
- Automatically adjusts based on listening habits
- Influences story recommendations

**Reading Streak**: Consecutive days of listening
- 🔥 icon shows current streak
- Encourages daily engagement

**Vocabulary**: Words learned through stories
- Tracks unique words encountered
- Shows vocabulary growth over time

**Life Readiness**: Overall preparedness score (0-100%)
- Based on life skills practiced in stories
- Increases as child engages with educational content

### Life Skills Practice

Progress bars showing practice in four areas:

1. **Wisdom**: Critical thinking, problem-solving
2. **Social**: Friendship, empathy, communication
3. **Money**: Financial literacy, saving, spending
4. **Balance**: Health, wellness, time management

**How it works**: Listening to stories with these themes increases the corresponding counter.

## Tips & Tricks

### Maximize Learning

1. **Discuss stories**: Talk with your child about what they learned
2. **Replay favorites**: Repetition reinforces lessons
3. **Try interactive stories**: Decision-making builds critical thinking
4. **Mix categories**: Balance fun and educational content

### Build Habits

1. **Set a routine**: Listen at the same time daily (bedtime works great)
2. **Track streaks**: Encourage daily listening for streak rewards
3. **Explore new categories**: Don't stick to just one type
4. **Use sleep timer**: Perfect for bedtime stories

### Optimize Experience

1. **Download for offline**: Save stories for car trips or flights
2. **Adjust speed**: Find the perfect pace for your child
3. **Use background play**: Listen while doing other activities
4. **Check related stories**: Discover similar content easily

## Troubleshooting

### Story Won't Play

**Solutions**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Restart the app
3. Clear app cache (Settings > Storage)
4. Update to latest version

### Audio Quality Issues

**Solutions**:
1. Check device volume
2. Test with headphones
3. Close other audio apps
4. Restart device

### Progress Not Saving

**Solutions**:
1. Ensure you're logged in
2. Check internet connection
3. Wait for sync indicator
4. Contact support if persists

### App Crashes

**Solutions**:
1. Update to latest version
2. Restart device
3. Clear app cache
4. Reinstall app (data is saved in cloud)
