I will give you a complete children's story written in {{LANGUAGE}}. It is narrated aloud in about {{DURATION_MINUTES}} minutes.

Your job is NOT to rewrite the story. Your job is to create a visual plan that uses the MINIMUM number of AI-generated images while still making the story feel visually rich and cinematic.

TARGET:
- Generate about {{TARGET_IMAGES}} UNIQUE base images for the whole story (never more than {{MAX_IMAGES}}).
- Reuse them with camera framing to reach AT LEAST {{MIN_SHOTS}} shots in the timeline (one visual change roughly every 20–40 seconds of narration).
- No two consecutive shots may be more than about {{MAX_GAP_WORDS}} words of story apart; long passages need extra shots (a new framing of the same image is fine).
- Camera framings: wide, medium, close_up. Movements: none, slow_zoom_in, slow_zoom_out, pan_left, pan_right, pan_up, pan_down.

COST RULE:
Do NOT generate a new image just because a different character speaks, the emotion changes, the camera should focus on another character, or a small action happens. Reuse the existing image and change the camera framing instead.
Generate a NEW image only for: a new location, a major action, a major magical transformation, important new visual information, a significant change in environment, or a climax moment the existing images cannot show.
If the story returns to a place already shown and it has not changed much, REUSE that image.

CHARACTERS (consistency is critical):
List EVERY character who appears in any image, including minor ones (parents, friends, animals).
Match the cultural setting of the story: names, places, homes, clothing and landscape must feel true to where the story is set. {{SETTING_HINT}}
Do not dress characters in clothing the story does not suggest (no raincoats, jeans or boots unless the story mentions them).
For each give: age, skin tone, hair, eyes, build, the outfit for each setting the story uses (for example day clothes and night clothes), important accessories, and for animals/fish their exact shape and colours.
Every image prompt must describe each character exactly as listed, using the outfit for that scene's setting.

COMPOSITION (the app crops and pans the images):
- Compose each base image slightly wider than necessary; keep important subjects away from the very edges.
- Place each important subject in a definite region of the picture so the app can zoom or pan to it on its own. Regions: left, center, right, top, bottom, top_left, top_right, bottom_left, bottom_right.
- The image prompt MUST state each subject's region in words, e.g. "Mia stands in the LEFT third; the fish tank with Fin is in the RIGHT third".
- Only include characters who are actually present at that moment of the story.

IMAGE PROMPTS:
For every base image write one detailed prompt covering characters (as listed), location, environment, action, facial expressions, the subject regions, lighting, mood and camera framing, in a soft painterly children's storybook style.
Never put text, subtitles, speech bubbles, labels or written words inside an image.
